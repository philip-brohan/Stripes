GloSAT
======



.. figure:: ../../../../GloSAT/GloSAT_sample_3x3.png
   :width: 95%
   :align: center
   :figwidth: 95%

   Monthly temperature anomalies (w.r.t. 1961-90) from the GloSAT dataset after regridding to a 0.25 degree latitude resolution. The vertical axis is latitude (south pole at the bottom, north pole at the top), and each pixel is from a randomly selected longitude and ensemble member. Grey areas show regions where GloSAT has no data.
