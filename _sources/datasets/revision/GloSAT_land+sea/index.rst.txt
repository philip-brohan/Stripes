GloSAT land and sea
===================

.. figure:: ../../../../GloSAT/LAT+MAT+Blended_sample_3x3.png
   :width: 95%
   :align: center
   :figwidth: 95%

   Monthly temperature anomalies (w.r.t. 1961-90) from GloSATLAT (top), GloSATMAT (middle), and GloSAT (bottom). In each case the vertical axis is latitude (south pole at the bottom, north pole at the top), and each pixel is from a randomly selected longitude and ensemble member. A 3x3 convolution is then applied. Grey areas show regions where the dataset has no data.
