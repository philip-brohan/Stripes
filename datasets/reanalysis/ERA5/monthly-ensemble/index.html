ERA5 monthly ensemble (enda)¶

The ERA5 Reanalysis, provides both a high-resolution flagship esitmate, and a 10-member ensemble, and is awesome as far as it goes. Unfortunately, as of Feb 2020, it only goes back to 1979 (an extension to 1950 is planned).

The reanalysis output is easily available, both hourly (3-hourly for the ensemble), and as monthly averages, from the Copernicus Climate Data Store. Here, I’m using the monthly averages from the ensemble.
